The Problem

Most link building actively hurts your site.

Bulk, irrelevant backlinks from link farms can trigger search penalties and rarely move rankings, but authority still matters in competitive fitness and wellness markets.

The Solution

We research genuine link opportunities, run relevant outreach, and pursue editorial and digital PR placements that build real authority, not just link count.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you buy links?

We focus on earned and outreach-based placements. Any paid placement costs are disclosed and agreed with you in advance, never bundled silently into your retainer.

How many links will I get?

2, 4, or 6 relevant authority links per month depending on your plan. We prioritize relevance and quality over hitting a raw number.

Can you guarantee specific publications?

No. Outreach and digital PR results depend on newsworthiness, timing, and publisher interest. We focus on maximizing genuine opportunities, not guaranteed placements.
