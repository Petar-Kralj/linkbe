FAQ

Common questions.

Do you handle multiple locations?

Yes. Local SEO for multi-location businesses is scoped based on how many locations you're targeting, confirmed during your growth call.

Can you get me reviews?

We build the review strategy and process; requesting reviews from real customers is something your team runs, since reviews must come from genuine customers to stay compliant with Google's policies.

Do I need a physical location for this to help?

Local SEO is built around a physical service area or storefront. Fully online fitness or wellness businesses are better served by our broader SEO content and technical services.
