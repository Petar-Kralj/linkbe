Technical SEO for fitness & wellness businesses.

Technical SEO ensures your website can be properly crawled, indexed, and understood by search engines: the foundation everything else in SEO is built on.

The Problem

Great content can't rank on a broken foundation.

Slow load times, broken crawl paths, messy site architecture, and missing structured data quietly cap how much traffic your site can ever earn, no matter how good the content is.

The Solution

Fix what's holding your site back, then keep it healthy.

We audit crawlability, indexing, architecture, and speed, prioritize by impact, and support implementation directly, not just a PDF of recommendations nobody acts on.

Deliverables

What's actually included.

  • Technical SEO audit
  • Website crawl analysis
  • Indexing & crawlability review
  • XML sitemap review
  • Robots.txt review
  • Broken link analysis
  • Canonical tag review
  • Website architecture review
  • Internal linking recommendations
  • Page speed recommendations
  • Mobile usability review
  • Structured data & implementation support
Who It's For

Any fitness or wellness business with an existing website, especially larger sites with multiple service pages, locations, or a recent redesign where technical issues are likely hiding.

Process

How we deliver it.

01

Full crawl, indexing, and architecture review to find what's actually limiting visibility.

02

Issues ranked by expected impact, not a 40-item checklist with no order.

03

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you make changes to my website directly?

On plans with implementation support, yes, for changes within our access (on-page elements, metadata, internal linking). Larger technical or development changes are supported alongside your developer.

How long does a technical SEO audit take?

Typically completed within the first two weeks of onboarding, then reviewed on an ongoing basis as part of your monthly plan.

Is technical SEO a one-time fix?

No. New pages, redesigns, and platform updates can reintroduce issues, which is why ongoing monitoring is included on Growth and Scale plans.
