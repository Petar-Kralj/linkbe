Questions before you hire us

What does a supplement SEO agency do?

It gets your product, ingredient and comparison pages ranking for the searches people make right before they buy, while keeping health claims within FDA and FTC rules. At Linkbe that covers keyword strategy, page rewrites, technical fixes, link building and reporting.

How much does supplement SEO cost?

It depends on catalogue size and how competitive your ingredients are. We send a fixed monthly quote after your free audit, so you know exactly what you get before committing.

How long until we see more orders from SEO?

Fixes and rewrites to existing product pages usually show movement in 4 to 8 weeks. New ingredient and comparison pages typically take 2 to 6 months to reach the top 3.

How do you keep supplement content compliant?

We use structure/function language, cite research for every benefit, add the FDA disclaimer where needed, and flag anything borderline for your sign-off before it goes live.

Can I hire you for link building only?

Yes. We place editorial links on real health and wellness sites, and you approve every site before we pitch it.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. After the first 3 months, work runs month to month, and you own everything we create.
